Agentic Game Development as a Verifiable Trajectory Data Engine for Scaling World Models

  • 논문/자료: Hugging Face Daily Papers · score 20

  • 핵심 내용: A common strategy for scaling world models is to train on more crawled video with more compute. We argue that this strategy is inefficient: scaling world models also requires a recursive data engine that offers grounded reward signals.
