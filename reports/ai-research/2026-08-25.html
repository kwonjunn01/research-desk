오늘은 스킵 3. ReWorld: An Interactive World Model with Long-Horizon Memory

  • 위치: HF #11 · 👍 13 · Inference / Efficiency · Zhifei Chen, Luozhou Wang · digest score 10

  • 핵심 내용: An interactive world model must follow the user's actions, remember the places it has shown, and stream in real time. The tension is structural: control wants a short horizon, memory wants an unbounded one.
  • 핵심 용어: KV cache = KV 캐시: 긴 대화/문서를 처리할 때 이전 token 정보를 저장하는 메모리; inference = 추론 실행: 학습이 끝난 모델을 실제 요청에 돌리는 단계
