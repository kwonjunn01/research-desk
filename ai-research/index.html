flowchart LR Paper[Paper / Repo / News] --> Topic[Topic Node] Topic --> Edge[Research Edge] Edge --> Lens[Signal Lens] Lens --> Weekly[Weekly Synthesis]

  • Research Edge: 어떤 topic이 다른 topic을 밀어주는지
