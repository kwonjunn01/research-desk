Snowflake (SNOW.US) — Monthly Momentum Daily Deep Report

KHJ Hermes Monthly Momentum Analyst Program · 2026-09-09 KST
관찰/검증용 리서치. 매수/매도 추천, 거래 지시, 목표가 제시가 아니다.
Judgment state: source incomplete / public provisional / judgment hold
핵심 보류 사유: EPS/consensus revision source 확인 필요. Public forward EPS proxy는 있으나 verified 30/60/90일 sell-side EPS·매출·마진 revision history와 원문 brokerage model bridge가 없다.

0. 20초 결론

한 줄 결론: Snowflake는 Q2 evidence-improved enterprise AI data-cloud re-rating candidate · Derivative Enabler 상단 · judgment hold다.

Q2 FY2027 1차 자료는 8월 보고서보다 더 강해졌다. SEC 8-K exhibit 기준 product revenue는 $1.4919B(+37% YoY), revenue는 $1.5468B(+35% YoY), NRR은 126%, $1M+ TTM product revenue 고객은 828(+27% YoY), Forbes Global 2000 고객은 829, RPO는 $9.004B(+30% YoY)다. 회사는 FY2027 product revenue guide를 $5.840B(+31%) → $6.070B(+36%)로 올렸고, non-GAAP operating margin guide도 13.5% → 14.5%로 상향했다. CoCo 9,100+ accounts, CoWork 5,800 accounts, Cortex AI Gateway/Natoma, 1H FY2027 GA product capabilities 330+는 “기업 AI/agent workflow가 governed data/context layer를 필요로 한다”는 thesis를 보강한다.

하지만 결론은 강한 reviewed re-rating 확정이 아니라 판단 보류다.

  1. 가격은 이미 Q2 beat-and-raise를 반영했다. 2026-09-09 Research Desk market cache 기준 SNOW는 $335.50, 20D +1.5%, 60D +39.8%, 52W position 81.6%, 52W high 대비 -12.8%다. 2026-09 queue 선정 당시에는 20D +13.0%, 60D +37.4%, 52W position 95.3%였다. 즉, Q2 발표 직후 급등이 있었지만 현재는 고점권에서 일부 식은 high-expectation zone이다.
  2. 사업 evidence는 real이다. Q2 product revenue acceleration, FY guide 상향, NRR 126%, 대형 고객 증가, AI account 증가, non-GAAP margin guide 상향은 단순 hype보다 강하다.
  3. valuation hurdle도 real이다. 2026-09-08 fundamentals cache 기준 market cap $118.96B, enterprise value $119.25B, forward PE 113.4x, P/B 55.3x, TTM revenue $5.43B, profit margin -20.1%, operating margin -17.0%다. “좋은 성장”만으로는 부족하고, product revenue/NRR/RPO/FCF/EPS revision이 계속 동행해야 한다.
  4. EPS/consensus revision source 확인 필요이다. Yahoo/YFinance public proxy는 forward EPS 2.97364, forward PE 113.39x를 보여주지만, 이는 verified 30/60/90일 sell-side revision history가 아니다. FactSet/Bloomberg/Visible Alpha/Refinitiv 또는 원문 sell-side estimate-change table은 연결되지 않았다.
  5. AI cost가 margin 논쟁으로 들어왔다. Q2 10-Q는 third-party cloud infrastructure expenses, including AI inference and GPUs가 Q2 FY2027 cost of product revenue의 약 75%였다고 설명한다. AI usage가 revenue를 늘리는 동시에 product gross margin을 압박할 수 있는 구조다.

Base case: Snowflake는 enterprise AI adoption의 data/context/governance layer에서 credibility가 올라간 Derivative Enabler다. 다만 price/valuation은 이미 Q2 acceleration과 AI Data Cloud narrative를 크게 반영했고, EPS/consensus revision source가 없어 judgment hold를 유지한다.

Publisher decision: PASS — provisional public report allowed. 단, 이 문서는 source-complete institutional conclusion이 아니라 source-boundary가 명시된 public provisional deep report다.

1.2 이번 daily deep report의 질문

이번 보고서의 질문은 “Snowflake가 AI 기능을 냈는가?”가 아니다. 이미 냈고, Q2 숫자도 좋아졌다. 질문은 더 좁다.

Q2 product revenue acceleration과 AI account 증가가 product consumption, NRR/RPO, gross margin, FCF, EPS/revenue revision으로 반복 번역되어 현재 valuation과 post-earnings price reflection을 정당화하는가?

현재 답은 네 문장이다.

  1. Company-primary evidence는 좋아졌다. Product revenue +37%, FY guide +36%로 상향, NRR 126%, RPO +30%, $1M+ customers 828은 momentum의 실체를 만든다.
  2. AI adoption signal도 더 구체화됐다. CoCo 9,100+ accounts, CoWork 5,800 accounts, Cortex AI Gateway/Natoma, 330+ GA product capabilities는 enterprise AI data/control-plane thesis를 강화한다.
  3. 가격과 valuation은 이미 높은 기대를 담고 있다. $335.50, 60D +39.8%, forward PE 113.4x, P/B 55.3x는 “다음 실적도 좋아야 하는” 구조다.
  4. Revision source가 닫혀 있다. Company guidance revision은 확인됐지만, verified sell-side EPS/revenue/margin revision tape가 없어 강한 결론은 금지한다.

2. Bottleneck-first thesis

2.1 병목 → 시스템 한계 → Snowflake edge

단계분석
병목Enterprise AI/agent workflow는 모델 API보다 먼저 데이터 품질, permissioning, lineage, governance, retrieval/context, compliance, audit, cost observability를 요구한다.
시스템 한계데이터가 silo에 있고 권한·품질·비용 통제가 약하면 agent가 production workflow로 못 들어간다. 또한 AI workload가 늘수록 cloud infra, inference/GPU cost, compute optimization이 product gross margin을 압박할 수 있다.
Breaker technology 후보governed data platform, AI Data Cloud, Cortex AI, Cortex AI Gateway, CoCo/CoWork, Snowpark, Marketplace/data sharing, MCP/tool-governance integration, cost-aware compute optimization.
Snowflake edge 후보consumption platform, large enterprise customer footprint, data sharing/marketplace network, first-party AI product surface, AWS/OpenAI/SAP/Natoma ecosystem, NRR/RPO visibility.
아직 부족한 것AI usage의 revenue attribution, current RPO conversion, NRR durability, product gross margin under AI inference/GPU cost, verified 30/60/90일 EPS/revenue/margin revision history.

2.2 왜 Bottleneck Breaker가 아니라 Derivative Enabler 상단인가

Snowflake는 AI infrastructure value chain에서 중요하지만, 오늘 기준 core physical bottleneck breaker로 보기는 어렵다.

  • GPU/HBM/power 병목: NVIDIA, HBM suppliers, advanced packaging, grid/power/cooling 업체가 더 직접적이다.
  • Snowflake의 직접 병목: enterprise data/context/governance/consumption predictability다.
  • 수익 전환 경로: AI workflow가 Snowflake 안에서 data preparation, query, AI function call, app/agent execution, marketplace/data sharing으로 실행될 때 product revenue consumption이 생긴다.
  • 상향 조건: AI accounts가 paid workload intensity와 product revenue/NRR/RPO/FCF/EPS revision으로 반복 확인되면 Indispensable Infrastructure 후보로 상향 검토 가능하다.
  • 하향 조건: AI launches가 account count와 marketing headline에 머물고 consumption/margin/revision으로 이어지지 않으면 Narrative Beta로 강등해야 한다.

2.3 핵심 thesis

Enterprise AI / Agentic workflow 증가
→ curated data · permission · governance · retrieval/context · audit · cost-control 병목
→ Snowflake AI Data Cloud / Cortex / Gateway / CoCo / CoWork / Snowpark / Marketplace
→ product revenue consumption · NRR · RPO · $1M+ customer expansion
→ product GM · operating margin · FCF · EPS/revenue revision
→ 가격 반영도 재평가

Thesis: Snowflake의 monthly momentum thesis는 “AI 기능이 많다”가 아니라 AI-era enterprise data/context bottleneck이 product consumption, NRR/RPO, margin, FCF, EPS revision으로 번역되는 속도다.

  1. AI workload consumption is real.

  1. Margin/FCF path is measurable.

Non-GAAP product GM, non-GAAP operating margin, FCF/adjusted FCF, AI infra cost ratio are visible checkpoints.

Bear case

  1. AI adoption metrics may be shallow.

CoCo 9,100+ accounts and CoWork 5,800 accounts are not the same as material paid consumption or EPS accretion.

  1. Consumption model can cut both ways.

Customers can optimize usage, reduce compute/storage, shorten committed durations, and slow consumption growth.

  1. Gross margin may be pressured by AI infra cost.

10-Q explicitly notes AI inference/GPU-related cloud infrastructure costs, which represented about 75% of cost of product revenue in Q2 FY2027.

  1. Valuation requires clean execution.

Forward PE 113.4x, P/B 55.3x, EV/Sales proxy around 22x leave little room for NRR/RPO/product-revenue disappointment.

  1. Competition is broadening.

AWS/Azure/GCP, Databricks, AI model providers, observability/application providers, and open data formats can reduce lock-in or pressure pricing.

  1. GAAP quality remains unresolved.

Q2 GAAP operating loss and net loss remain material. Non-GAAP execution is improving, but SBC/amortization/adjustments must be monitored.

Invalidation conditions

Snowflake monthly momentum thesis should be downgraded if any of the following occur:

  • Product revenue growth decelerates materially below guide while price remains high.
  • NRR moves down and management attributes it to customer optimization/budget control.
  • RPO growth/current conversion weakens beyond normal seasonality.
  • AI accounts rise but management does not show consumption/revenue/customer expansion linkage.
  • Product gross margin or FCF margin deteriorates because AI/cloud infra costs rise.
  • Verified EPS/consensus revision history shows no upward revision despite price re-rating.
  • Public/source-pack review reveals AI narrative is mostly product-launch marketing without revenue bridge.

12. Risks and blind spots

  1. EPS/consensus revision source 확인 필요

Public EPS proxy is not enough. Forward EPS and forward PE snapshot cannot prove 30/60/90일 revision direction.

  1. Brokerage model bridge missing

Original sell-side reports, estimate tables, and model changes are 확인 필요. Public headlines are discovery only.

  1. AI account metric is internally classified

CoCo/CoWork account count is useful but not an audited revenue metric. It must connect to paid workload intensity and gross margin.

  1. RPO can mislead without consumption timing

RPO is not pure backlog-to-revenue guarantee. Current portion, contract duration, and consumption schedule matter.

  1. Consumption optimization risk

Snowflake’s platform improvements can reduce compute required for the same workload. If new workloads do not offset efficiency, revenue growth can slow.

  1. Competition / vertical integration risk

Hyperscalers and AI model providers can integrate upward/downward into data management and agent tooling. Snowflake can be partner and competitor with the same entities.

  1. GAAP vs non-GAAP gap

GAAP operating loss and net loss remain material. SBC, amortization, acquisition-related costs, and repurchases need ongoing quality-of-earnings review.

  1. Price reflexivity

When a software stock trades at a high forward multiple, small guidance language changes can dominate actual long-term fundamentals.

  1. Market data field caveat

Latest price cache is useful for 20D/60D/52W, but the single-day change field should be QA-checked against Yahoo chart close sequence before being used in future reports.
