Coherent (COHR.US) — Monthly Momentum Deep Report

작성일: 2026-05-24 KST
프로그램: KHJ Hermes Monthly Momentum Analyst Program
성격: 관찰/분석/검증 보고서. 매수/매도 추천 또는 목표가 제시가 아니다.
판단 상태: Crowded Re-rating candidate · Judgment hold / source incomplete
핵심 caveat: EPS/consensus revision 이력 확인 필요 · 판단 보류 — Nasdaq public EPS forecast와 4-week revision count는 확인했지만, FactSet/Bloomberg/Visible Alpha 같은 paid/verified 30/60/90일 consensus revision time-series와 원문 sell-side model bridge는 연결되지 않았다.

0. 20초 결론

한 줄 결론 — Crowded Re-rating candidate · 판단 보류.

Coherent는 이번 monthly momentum queue에서 단순 “AI 광통신 테마주”가 아니라 AI factory가 커질수록 전기적 interconnect·copper reach·power-per-bit 한계가 optical interconnect, silicon photonics, InP laser, 800G/1.6T transceiver, CPO/NPO로 이동하는 병목을 검증해야 하는 회사다. 회사의 2026년 5월 6일 FY3Q26 1차 자료는 강하다. Q3 매출은 $1.806B, +20.5% YoY, GAAP gross margin은 37.7%, +243bps YoY, non-GAAP gross margin은 39.6%, +105bps YoY, GAAP diluted EPS는 $0.97, non-GAAP EPS는 $1.41로 제시됐다. Datacenter & Communications 매출은 $1.362B, +41% YoY였고, 회사는 Q4 FY2026 매출 가이던스를 $1.91B~$2.05B, non-GAAP EPS 가이던스를 $1.52~$1.72로 제시했다.

그래도 이 보고서의 결론은 강한 확정이 아니라 판단 보류다.

  1. 가격 반영이 이미 크다. Research Desk 2026-05-24 market cache 기준 COHR은 $377.57, 52주 범위 위치 89.6%, 20D +12.3%, 60D +50.9%, 52주 고점 대비 -8.6%다. 60D re-rating이 이미 컸고, Nasdaq public quote도 2026-05-21 종가 $377.57를 표시한다.
  2. NVIDIA partnership은 강한 company-specific signal이지만 concentration/capex/working-capital risk도 만든다. 2026년 3월 2일 8-K와 Q3 10-Q는 NVIDIA의 $2B investment, multi-year strategic agreement, multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment, future access/capacity rights를 확인한다. 동시에 10-Q는 이 capacity agreement가 2030년까지 equipment, labor, working capital 투자를 요구할 수 있고 revenue concentration, gross margin, capex에 영향을 줄 수 있다고 적었다.
  3. 성장 품질은 좋지만 cash conversion은 아직 부담이다. Q3 reported revenue와 margin은 좋아졌지만, FY2026 9개월 operating cash flow는 $10M으로 전년 $503M보다 크게 낮다. 회사는 inventory 증가가 higher revenue levels를 지원하기 위한 working-capital 사용이라고 설명한다. 즉 수요가 강하다는 신호이면서도, capacity ramp가 현금·재고·마진 리스크를 동반한다는 뜻이다.
  4. EPS/consensus revision 이력 확인 필요 · 판단 보류 상태다. Nasdaq public proxy는 FY2026 EPS $4.70, FY2027 $7.28, FY2028 $10.99와 FY2027 4-week upward revision 8/0을 보여주지만, verified revision history가 없어 “주가 상승을 EPS revision이 충분히 따라왔다/못 따라왔다”는 강한 결론은 보류한다.

따라서 현재 가장 현실적인 판정은: Coherent는 AI optical infrastructure에서 실제 primary-source evidence와 NVIDIA-linked capacity signal이 있는 re-rating 후보지만, 이미 가격이 많이 반영됐고, 2026~2027년 매출·gross margin·capex·working capital·EPS revision이 함께 따라와야 한다. 높은 우선순위 관찰 후보, 강한 결론 보류.

핵심 framing

Coherent의 thesis는 “AI라서 오른다”가 아니다. 더 정확한 chain은 다음이다.

flowchart LR A[AI factory scale-out: training, inference, agents] --> B[Bandwidth density / power-per-bit bottleneck] B --> C[Advanced optics: lasers, InP, transceivers, CPO/NPO] C --> D[Coherent capacity + NVIDIA-linked product access] D --> E[Datacenter & Communications revenue] E --> F[Gross margin / working capital / capex] F --> G[EPS and consensus revision] G --> H[Price reflection or disappointment]

여기서 가장 중요한 질문은 AI optical demand가 Coherent의 Datacenter & Communications 매출, gross margin, EPS, cash conversion으로 얼마나 빨리 번역되는가다. Coherent는 GPU/HBM 공급 자체를 해결하는 회사는 아니지만, AI cluster가 더 커지고 optical I/O가 필요해질수록 물리 인프라 병목의 한가운데로 들어온다.

3.3 NVIDIA strategic partnership driver

2026년 3월 2일 8-K/press release와 FY3Q26 10-Q는 Coherent의 company-specific signal을 강화한다.

  • NVIDIA는 Coherent에 $2B를 투자했다.
  • 계약은 multi-year strategic agreement로, advanced optics technologies, manufacturing capacity, R&D, next-generation AI infrastructure를 대상으로 한다.
  • 계약은 non-exclusive이며, NVIDIA multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment와 future access/capacity rights for advanced laser and optical networking products를 포함한다고 공시됐다.
  • 8-K는 NVIDIA가 access하는 product families가 co-packaged optics와 next-generation AI infrastructure에 관련된다고 설명했다.
  • 10-Q는 이 arrangement가 2030년까지 equipment, labor, working capital 투자를 필요로 할 수 있고, revenue concentration, gross margin, capital expenditures에 영향을 줄 수 있다고 밝혔다.

해석: 이것은 단순 뉴스가 아니라 customer/partner가 capacity와 technology roadmap에 자본을 같이 넣은 신호다. 그러나 동시에 한 고객/파트너 축이 커질수록 bargaining power, execution schedule, capex timing, working capital risk도 커진다.

4. Bottleneck-first thesis

4.1 병목 → 시스템 한계 → breaker technology → 회사 edge

LayerCoherent에 해당하는 내용
Physical/system bottleneckAI cluster가 커질수록 copper SerDes와 전기적 interconnect는 거리, 전력, 열, latency, bandwidth density에서 부담이 커진다.
Breaker/enabler technologyInP lasers, silicon photonics, advanced laser/optical networking products, 800G/1.6T/3.2T transceivers, DCI transceivers, transport/transmission optical components, CPO/NPO engines.
Company edgebroad/deep photonics stack, transceiver/module/system capability, global manufacturing scale, InP output expansion, NVIDIA partnership and purchase/capacity rights.
Money pathhyperscaler/NVIDIA demand → advanced optics capacity reservation/order → component/transceiver shipment → D&C revenue → gross margin → EPS revision → price reflection.
Key riskdemand visibility가 capacity/capex/working-capital commitment로 먼저 나타나고, realized revenue/margin/cash conversion은 뒤따라오지 못할 수 있다.

4.2 왜 “Bottleneck Breaker”가 아니라 “Indispensable Infrastructure”인가

Coherent는 AI optical bottleneck을 완화하는 데 매우 중요하지만, 아직 이 보고서에서는 Tier를 Indispensable Infrastructure로 둔다.

  • Bull evidence: NVIDIA partnership, $2B investment, multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment, advanced optics capacity rights, Datacenter & Communications +41% YoY.
  • 그러나 Coherent가 GPU/HBM/advanced packaging 병목을 직접 해결하는 것은 아니다.
  • CPO/NPO/1.6T/3.2T roadmap은 강하지만, 제품별 customer qualification, shipment volume, ASP, gross margin, capex burden은 더 확인해야 한다.
  • presentation의 incremental SAM과 roadmap은 company-forward-looking 자료라 독립 시장 데이터와 고객별 order evidence가 필요하다.

따라서 “좋은 AI optics story”가 아니라 optical infrastructure가 실제 revenue/margin/EPS revision으로 변하는지가 core thesis다. 그 전까지 Bottleneck Breaker로 완전 승격하면 가격 반영을 과소평가할 위험이 있다.

5. Business model → money path

5.1 Money path map

Coherent의 돈이 들어오는 단위는 다음이다.

  1. Datacenter & Communications revenue: FY3Q26 $1.362B, +41% YoY. AI datacenter demand와 DCI/scale-across/traditional telecom demand가 모두 들어간다.
  2. Transceivers / optical components / laser capacity: 회사 overview는 lasers, transceivers, optical/optoelectronic devices, modules, systems, engineered materials를 핵심 제품군으로 설명한다.
  3. InP and advanced optics capacity: presentation은 InP output expansion, 400G/lane links, 1.6T/3.2T, DCI transceivers, optical components, thermal/CPO/NPO roadmap을 강조한다. 이는 2026~2027 revenue bridge의 핵심 후보지만, 독립 검증이 필요하다.
  4. NVIDIA partnership economics: purchase commitment와 future access/capacity rights는 demand visibility를 높인다. 그러나 revenue recognition schedule, pricing, gross margin, capex burden은 아직 공시 숫자로 분해되지 않았다.
  5. Industrial segment optionality: Industrial은 semiconductor capital equipment demand를 일부 반영하지만, FY3Q26에는 divestitures로 매출이 감소했다. AI optical thesis의 중심은 D&C다.

5.2 Quality of revenue

Revenue quality 판단에는 네 가지가 필요하다.

  • Segment concentration quality: D&C가 전체 매출의 약 75%로 커졌다. AI optical thesis와의 직접성은 커졌지만 cyclicality와 customer/project concentration도 커질 수 있다.
  • Margin durability: gross margin 개선이 higher volume, input cost reduction, cycle-time efficiency, yield improvement, pricing optimization에서 왔다. 이 개선이 capacity ramp에도 유지되는지 봐야 한다.
  • Cash conversion: FY2026 9개월 operating cash flow는 $10M으로 전년 $503M 대비 낮다. 회사는 inventory build를 higher revenue levels 지원으로 설명한다. 수요 신호와 cash burden이 동시에 존재한다.
  • Balance-sheet flexibility: cash/equivalents $1.593B, short-term investments $825M, total debt $3.194B. NVIDIA investment가 capacity ramp를 지원하지만, debt/capex/working-capital 조합은 계속 확인해야 한다.

  2. NVIDIA partnership이 Coherent의 customer validation과 capacity visibility를 크게 높인다.
  3. D&C revenue +41% YoY가 일회성이 아니라 2026~2027 성장 구간의 시작이다.
  4. Q4 revenue $1.91B~$2.05B와 non-GAAP EPS $1.52~$1.72가 달성 가능하다.

이 기대가 맞아도 주가가 더 오른다고 단정할 수 없다. 이미 60D +50.9% re-rating이 있었기 때문에, 다음 판단은 기대가 더 빨리 올라가는지와 cash/margin burden이 드러나지 않는지가 좌우한다.

6.3 Revision이 더 올라가려면 필요한 숫자

  1. Q4 revenue $1.91B~$2.05B 달성 또는 상회: guide midpoint를 넘는지 확인.
  2. Q4 non-GAAP gross margin 39~41% 유지: capacity ramp와 product mix가 margin을 해치지 않는지 확인.
  3. D&C revenue growth 지속: FY3Q26 +41% YoY가 단발성인지, 2026~2027 optical cycle인지 구분.
  4. NVIDIA-linked product conversion: purchase commitment/access rights가 제품별 order, shipment, revenue, margin으로 전환되는지.
  5. InP/optical capacity ramp: Sherman, Texas InP capacity expansion과 industry-wide shortage 완화/심화가 숫자로 확인되는지.
  6. Operating cash flow recovery: inventory build가 revenue conversion으로 돌아오는지, capex/working capital이 계속 누르는지.
  7. Verified consensus revision: public EPS proxy뿐 아니라 paid/verified 30/60/90일 revision source에서 FY2026/FY2027 상향이 확인되는지.

6.4 Revision이 꺾이는 경로

  • Q4 revenue가 guide 하단에 머물거나 FY2027 ramp commentary가 약해진다.
  • gross margin이 capacity ramp, pricing, yield, product mix 때문에 39% 아래로 밀린다.
  • NVIDIA-related capacity investment가 매출보다 capex/working capital 부담으로 먼저 나타난다.
  • inventory build가 demand visibility가 아니라 excess/obsolescence risk로 재해석된다.
  • optical interconnect value capture가 Coherent보다 Broadcom/Marvell/DSP, hyperscaler internal design, private photonics players, lower-margin pluggables 쪽으로 이동한다.
  • public EPS revisions가 멈추거나 down revisions가 증가한다.
  • 주가는 높은데 direct shipment/order/backlog disclosure가 부족해 narrative beta로 재분류된다.

8. Base / Upside / Downside scenarios

Base case — 가장 현실적인 현재 가정

Thesis: Coherent는 AI factory scale-out이 optical interconnect와 photonics capacity demand로 전환되는 구간에서 re-rating을 받았다. FY3Q26은 D&C revenue +41% YoY, gross margin expansion, Q4 guide, NVIDIA partnership이라는 강한 evidence를 제공한다. 다만 가격은 이미 많이 반영됐고, verified EPS revision, cash conversion, capex/working-capital, product/customer concentration gate가 남아 있다.

Evidence for Base:

  • FY3Q26 revenue $1.806B / +20.5% YoY.
  • D&C revenue $1.362B / +41% YoY.
  • GAAP gross margin 37.7%, +243bps YoY; non-GAAP gross margin 39.6%, +105bps YoY.
  • Q4 revenue guide $1.91B~$2.05B and non-GAAP EPS guide $1.52~$1.72.
  • NVIDIA $2B investment, multi-year strategic agreement, purchase commitment, capacity/access rights.
  • Nasdaq public forecast shows FY2027 EPS $7.28 with 4-week upward revisions 8/0.

Evidence against / limits:

  • Price: 52W position 89.6%, 60D +50.9%.
  • Forward P/E 43.7x, trailing P/E 169x, EV/EBITDA 53.7x.
  • 9M operating cash flow $10M vs prior-year $503M, inventory build and capex burden.
  • NVIDIA agreement may increase capex, working capital, concentration, gross margin risk.
  • EPS/consensus revision 이력 확인 필요 · 판단 보류.
  • Original sell-side model and independent optical market share/ASP datasets are 접근 제한/확인 필요.

Base judgment: Crowded re-rating candidate. 관찰 지속, 강한 확정 결론 보류.

Upside case

Thesis: AI datacenter scale-out accelerates optical interconnect adoption faster than expected. Coherent converts NVIDIA-linked access/capacity rights and broader AI optics demand into product shipments, D&C revenue, and margin expansion. InP capacity expansion relieves supply bottlenecks without destroying pricing, and FY2027/FY2028 EPS estimates move higher.

Triggers:

  • Q4 FY2026 revenue is at/above the high end of $1.91B~$2.05B.
  • non-GAAP gross margin stays around 40% despite capacity ramp.
  • D&C growth remains well above company average and management gives product-specific order/shipment evidence.
  • NVIDIA-related purchase commitment begins to show as recognized revenue or backlog/order detail, not only strategic language.
  • operating cash flow improves as inventory converts to revenue.
  • verified consensus source confirms FY2027/FY2028 EPS upward revisions.
  • company provides clearer visibility on 1.6T/3.2T/CPO/NPO revenue timing and customer qualification.

What would prove it: market begins to value Coherent not as a one-time AI optics trade, but as a multi-year photonics infrastructure compounder where capacity, revenue, margin, and EPS revision move together.

Downside case

Thesis: FY3Q26 and NVIDIA partnership are real, but the stock has already priced a near-perfect AI optical cycle. If product qualification, capacity ramp, cash conversion, or gross margin disappoints, the market may reframe Coherent from “critical AI optics bottleneck enabler” to “crowded capex derivative with heavy working-capital and multiple risk.”

Triggers:

  • Q4 revenue guide is merely met but gross margin weakens or FY2027 commentary is less forceful.
  • inventory/capex/working capital continue to consume cash without visible revenue conversion.
  • NVIDIA demand creates concentration or pricing pressure rather than margin expansion.
  • CPO/NPO/1.6T/3.2T adoption takes longer than market narrative assumes.
  • public EPS revisions stop, down revisions appear, or verified source does not support the Nasdaq public proxy.
  • optical value capture shifts toward lower-margin pluggables, merchant silicon/DSP, or hyperscaler internalization.
  • share price stays near upper 52W range while evidence quality weakens.

What would confirm downside: price remains high but Q4/FY2027 numbers fail to accelerate, gross margin slips below the expected range, and cash conversion remains weak because capacity build absorbs the demand signal before shareholders see EPS/FCF revision.

9. Bull case / Bear case / Invalidation

Bull case

  1. Primary-source growth is real. FY3Q26 revenue +20.5% YoY and D&C revenue +41% YoY.
  2. Margins expanded. GAAP gross margin +243bps YoY; non-GAAP gross margin +105bps YoY.
  3. Q4 guide implies continued growth. Revenue guide $1.91B~$2.05B is above Q3 $1.806B.
  4. NVIDIA signal is unusually company-specific. $2B investment plus purchase/capacity rights validates Coherent as a strategic optical supplier.
  5. Technology stack is broad. Lasers, transceivers, modules, systems, engineered materials, InP capacity, CPO/NPO roadmap create multiple ways to participate in AI optical demand.
  6. Balance sheet flexibility improved. cash/equivalents + short-term investments increased, total debt decreased from June 2025.
  7. Public EPS proxy points upward. Nasdaq shows FY2027 EPS $7.28 and 4-week upward revisions, though unverified.

Bear case

  1. Price is crowded. 60D +50.9% and 52W position 89.6% make evidence threshold high.
  2. Valuation burden is high. Forward P/E 43.7x and EV/EBITDA 53.7x require sustained revision.
  3. Cash conversion is weak so far. 9M operating cash flow $10M despite strong earnings, mainly due to inventory build.
  4. Capacity ramp is double-edged. It supports demand but can pressure capex, labor, working capital, and gross margin.
  5. NVIDIA dependency risk. A strategic partner can validate demand but also concentrate revenue schedule and bargaining power.
  6. Technology timing risk. CPO/NPO/1.6T/3.2T adoption may lag market enthusiasm.
  7. Consensus quality is incomplete. Public EPS forecast is useful but EPS/consensus revision 이력 확인 필요 · 판단 보류 remains.
  8. Sell-side/industry source pack is incomplete. Original reports and independent paid optical market datasets are blocked/attempted.

Invalidation conditions

This report’s constructive observation is downgraded if two or more occur:

  • Q4 FY2026 revenue is below $1.91B or management lowers/softens FY2027 demand commentary.
  • non-GAAP gross margin falls below 39% without clear temporary explanation.
  • D&C growth decelerates sharply or AI datacenter demand does not translate into order/shipment detail.
  • NVIDIA-related agreement remains strategic language without visible revenue/backlog/order conversion.
  • operating cash flow fails to recover as inventory continues to rise.
  • capex/working capital and labor ramp reduce FCF expectations more than revenue upside improves them.
  • public or verified EPS revisions turn down for FY2027/FY2028.
  • customer qualification, yield, or manufacturing capacity targets slip.
  • price remains near the upper 52W range while evidence quality weakens.

10. Risks and blind spots

10.1 Source risks

  • Brokerage source boundary: public snippets show analyst/valuation debate, but original reports and model bridges are 확인 필요. This report does not use ratings or target prices.
  • Consensus source boundary: Nasdaq public EPS forecast and earnings surprise were checked, but EPS/consensus revision 이력 확인 필요 · 판단 보류 remains. Public 4-week counts are not a replacement for verified 30/60/90일 revision time-series.
  • Industry source gap: independent optical transceiver/CPO/InP capacity, ASP, market share, customer qualification datasets are 확인 필요.
  • Transcript limitation: earnings release/presentation/10-Q were parsed; full call Q&A nuance remains attempted/not fully parsed.
  • Market data timing: Research Desk cache, Nasdaq quote, and Yahoo/fundamentals snapshots have different timestamps and definitions.

10.2 Business risks

  • Customer/partner concentration: NVIDIA partnership is a strong signal but can create concentration and execution dependency.
  • Manufacturing ramp: InP/optical capacity expansion requires equipment, labor, working capital, yield, and supply-chain execution.
  • Inventory risk: inventory increased materially; if demand timing slips, working capital can become overhang.
  • Margin mix: datacenter growth may carry strong volume but different pricing, product mix, and ramp cost characteristics.
  • Technology substitution: CPO/NPO, pluggables, merchant DSP, hyperscaler internalization, and private photonics players can change value capture.
  • Tariff/export controls: 10-Q says Q3 impact was not material, but global trade disruption can still create revenue reduction, cost increases, or production delays.
  • Industrial segment noise: divestitures and Industrial cyclicality can obscure the cleaner AI optical story.

10.3 Analytical blind spots

  • No paid consensus revision time-series.
  • No original sell-side model.
  • No independent current optical market share / ASP / capacity dataset.
  • No full earnings call Q&A extraction.
  • No customer-by-customer NVIDIA/hyperscaler deployment schedule.
  • No product-level revenue split for 800G/1.6T/3.2T/CPO/NPO.
  • No price target or buy/sell framework by design.
