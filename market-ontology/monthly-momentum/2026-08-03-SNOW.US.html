Snowflake (SNOW.US) — Monthly Momentum Daily Deep Report

KHJ Hermes Monthly Momentum Analyst Program · 2026-08-03 KST
관찰/검증용 리서치. 거래 실행 지시 또는 가격 목적값 제시가 아니다.
Judgment state: source incomplete / public provisional / judgment hold
핵심 보류 사유: EPS/consensus revision source 확인 필요. Public forward EPS proxy는 있으나 verified 30/60/90일 sell-side EPS·매출·마진 revision history와 원문 brokerage model bridge가 없다.

0. 20초 결론

한 줄 결론: Snowflake는 Crowded enterprise AI data-cloud re-rating candidate · Derivative Enabler 후보 · judgment hold다.

Q1 FY2027 회사 1차 자료는 강하다. Snowflake는 product revenue $1.33B(+34% YoY), revenue $1.39B(+33% YoY), net revenue retention 126%, $1M+ trailing product revenue customer 779(+29% YoY), remaining performance obligations $9.21B(+38% YoY), FY2027 product revenue guide $5.84B(+31%)를 제시했다. Cortex Code, Snowflake Intelligence, Snowpark, Marketplace, AWS/OpenAI/SAP/Natoma/MCP surface는 “AI agent가 기업 데이터·권한·governance 위에서 돌아가려면 Snowflake 같은 data/context layer가 필요하다”는 thesis를 뒷받침한다.

하지만 이번 monthly momentum 판단은 강한 reviewed re-rating 확정이 아니라 보류다. 이유는 네 가지다.

  1. 가격 반영이 매우 앞서 있다. 최신 Research Desk market-sources-2026-08-03.json 기준 SNOW는 $293.28, 20D +12.7%, 60D +107.0%, 52W position 94.1%, 52W high 대비 -3.6%다. 2026-07-13 deep report 당시 $261.45 대비 약 +12.2% 추가 상승했다. 이는 “숨은 저평가”가 아니라 높은 기대가 이미 가격에 반영된 crowded observation zone이다.
  2. 사업 evidence는 실재하지만, 증명해야 할 변수는 더 좁아졌다. 이제 질문은 “AI 기능을 출시했는가?”가 아니다. 질문은 AI account와 agent/data-cloud narrative가 product revenue consumption, NRR, RPO/current conversion, gross margin, FCF, EPS/revenue revision으로 반복 확인되는가다.
  3. valuation hurdle이 높다. market-fundamentals-cache.json public snapshot 기준 market cap $101.65B, forward PE 109.0x, P/B 52.4x, TTM revenue $5.03B, market-cap/sales와 EV/sales는 약 20.2x다. 이 수치는 투자 판단이 아니라, execution/revision 증거가 조금만 약해져도 가격이 민감해질 수 있는 기대치 구조를 뜻한다.
  4. EPS/consensus revision source 확인 필요이다. Yahoo Finance Analysis route는 이번 접근에서 HTTP 503, Nasdaq earnings route는 timeout, FactSet/Bloomberg/Visible Alpha/Refinitiv 또는 원문 sell-side estimate-change table은 연결되지 않았다. 따라서 “아직 덜 반영됐다” 또는 “이미 끝났다”는 강한 결론을 금지한다.

Base case: Snowflake는 enterprise AI adoption의 data/context/governance layer에서 credible Derivative Enabler다. 그러나 가격은 이미 Q1 recovery와 agentic enterprise narrative를 상당히 반영했고, EPS/revision source gate가 닫혀 있어 judgment hold를 유지한다.

Publisher decision: PASS — provisional public report allowed. 단, 이 문서는 reviewed institutional conclusion이 아니라 source-boundary가 명시된 public provisional deep report다.

1.2 이번 daily deep report의 질문

이번 보고서의 질문은 “Snowflake가 좋은 회사인가?”가 아니다. 질문은 더 좁다.

AI Data Cloud와 Agentic Enterprise narrative가 product revenue, NRR/RPO, gross margin, FCF, EPS/revenue revision으로 번역되는 속도가 현재 60D +107% re-rating과 52W 94% 가격 위치를 정당화할 만큼 빠른가?

현재 답은 네 문장이다.

  1. Demand/product signal은 좋아졌다. Q1 product revenue +34%, NRR 126%, RPO +38%, AI capability account 13,600+는 narrative-only 반론을 약화한다.
  2. Monetization bridge는 아직 불완전하다. AI account count와 paid workload intensity/revenue attribution은 다르다.
  3. 가격은 이미 높은 기대를 반영했다. 60D +107%, 52W position 94.1%, forward PE 109x는 “다음 숫자”의 허들을 높인다.
  4. Revision source가 없다. 회사 guide 상향은 확인됐지만, verified consensus EPS/revenue/margin revision history가 없어 강한 결론을 보류한다.

2. Bottleneck-first thesis

2.1 병목 → 물리/시스템 한계 → Snowflake edge

단계분석
병목Enterprise AI/agent workflow는 모델 API보다 먼저 데이터 품질, 권한, lineage, governance, retrieval/context, compliance, audit trail을 요구한다.
물리/시스템 한계데이터가 silo에 있고 권한·품질·비용 통제가 약하면 agent가 production workflow로 못 들어간다. 또한 AI workload가 늘수록 cloud infra, inference/GPU cost, compute optimization이 gross margin을 압박할 수 있다.
Breaker technology 후보governed data platform, AI Data Cloud, Cortex AI, Snowpark, Snowflake Intelligence, marketplace/data sharing, MCP/tool governance, cost-aware compute optimization.
Snowflake edge 후보consumption platform, large enterprise customer footprint, data sharing/marketplace network, first-party AI product surface, AWS/OpenAI/SAP/Natoma ecosystem.
아직 부족한 것AI usage의 revenue attribution, current RPO conversion, NRR trend, product gross margin under AI inference cost, verified 30/60/90일 EPS/revenue revision history.

2.2 왜 Bottleneck Breaker가 아니라 Derivative Enabler인가

Snowflake는 AI infrastructure value chain에서 중요하지만, 오늘 기준 core physical bottleneck breaker로 보기는 어렵다.

  • GPU/HBM/power 병목: NVIDIA, HBM suppliers, advanced packaging, grid/power/cooling 업체가 더 직접적이다.
  • Snowflake의 직접 병목: enterprise data/context/governance/consumption predictability다.
  • 수익 전환 경로: AI workflow가 Snowflake 안에서 data preparation, query, compute, app/agent execution, marketplace/data sharing으로 실행될 때 product revenue consumption이 생긴다.
  • tier 해석: Snowflake는 AI workload adoption을 enterprise data consumption으로 번역하는 Derivative Enabler다. Indispensable Infrastructure로 올리려면 AI product usage가 RPO/NRR/product revenue/FCF/EPS revision으로 반복 확인되어야 한다.

2.3 핵심 thesis

Enterprise AI / Agentic workflow 증가
→ curated data · permission · governance · retrieval/context · audit 병목
→ Snowflake AI Data Cloud / Cortex / Snowpark / Snowflake Intelligence / Marketplace
→ product revenue consumption · NRR · RPO · $1M+ customer expansion
→ gross margin · operating margin · FCF · EPS/revenue revision
→ 가격 반영도 재평가

Thesis: Snowflake의 monthly momentum thesis는 “AI 기능이 많다”가 아니라 AI-era enterprise data/context bottleneck이 product consumption, NRR/RPO, margin, FCF, EPS revision으로 번역되는가다.

Bull case

  1. AI workload consumption is real.

Product revenue +34%, NRR 126%, FY guide 상향은 enterprise data-cloud workload가 revenue로 번역되고 있음을 보여준다.

  1. Large-customer expansion supports durability.

  1. Snowflake has agent/governance relevance.

Cortex Code, Snowflake Intelligence, Natoma/MCP, AWS/OpenAI/SAP partnerships place Snowflake near the data/context layer of agentic workflows.

  1. Margin/FCF path is visible enough to monitor.

Non-GAAP product GM 75.1%, non-GAAP op margin 11.9%, FCF margin 16.7%, FY adjusted FCF margin guide 23% create measurable checkpoints.

Bear case

  1. AI adoption metrics may be shallow.

13,600+ AI accounts and 7,100+ Cortex Code accounts are not the same as material paid consumption or EPS accretion.

  1. Consumption model can cut both ways.

Customers can optimize usage, reduce storage, shorten committed durations, and slow consumption growth.

  1. Gross margin may be pressured by AI infra cost.

10-Q explicitly notes AI inference/GPU-related cloud infrastructure costs. New product capabilities may lack scale economies.

  1. Valuation requires clean execution.

Forward PE 109.0x, P/B 52.4x, EV/Sales 약 20.2x leave little room for NRR/RPO/product-revenue disappointment.

  1. Competition is broadening.

AWS/Azure/GCP, Databricks, AI model providers, observability/application providers, and open data formats can reduce lock-in or pressure pricing.

Invalidation conditions

Snowflake monthly momentum thesis should be downgraded if any of the following occur:

  • Product revenue growth decelerates materially below guide while price remains high.
  • NRR moves down and management attributes it to customer optimization/budget control.
  • RPO growth/current conversion weakens beyond normal seasonality.
  • AI accounts rise but management does not show consumption/revenue/customer expansion linkage.
  • Product gross margin or FCF margin deteriorates because AI/cloud infra costs rise.
  • Verified EPS/consensus revision history shows no upward revision despite price re-rating.
  • Public/source-pack review reveals AI narrative is mostly product-launch marketing without revenue bridge.

12. Risks and blind spots

  1. EPS/consensus revision source 확인 필요

Public EPS proxy is not enough. Forward EPS and forward PE snapshot cannot prove 30/60/90일 revision direction.

  1. Brokerage model bridge missing

Original sell-side reports, estimate tables, and model changes are 확인 필요. Public headlines are discovery only.

  1. AI account metric is internally classified

AI capability account count is useful but not an audited revenue metric. It must connect to paid workload intensity and gross margin.

  1. RPO can mislead without consumption timing

RPO is not pure backlog-to-revenue guarantee. Current portion, contract duration, and consumption schedule matter.

  1. Consumption optimization risk

Snowflake’s platform improvements can reduce compute required for the same workload. If new workloads do not offset efficiency, revenue growth can slow.

  1. Competition / vertical integration risk

Hyperscalers and AI model providers can integrate upward/downward into data management and agent tooling. Snowflake can be partner and competitor with the same entities.

  1. GAAP vs non-GAAP gap

GAAP operating loss and net loss remain material. SBC, amortization, acquisition-related costs, and repurchases need ongoing quality-of-earnings review.

  1. Price reflexivity

When a software stock trades at a high forward multiple and near 52W high, small guidance language changes can dominate actual long-term fundamentals.

  1. CEO PSU milestone misread risk

2026-07-16 8-K compensation milestones can show board-level long-term value-creation ambition, but they are not operating evidence and not an investment price objective.
